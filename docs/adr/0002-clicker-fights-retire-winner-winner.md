# Clicker fights retire Winner winner from its current holders

Winner winner needs every regular badge, and winning a clicker fight is now one of them. The migration therefore takes Winner winner away from everyone who holds it and gives each of them the one-off legacy badge "Ich war schon mal winner" (100 XP) instead. They earn Winner winner back by winning a fight. We rejected keeping it for current holders because it would leave two classes of Winner winner, and rejected a retroactive fight badge because nobody could then be the first fight winner.

## Consequences

- Holders lose a net 400 XP (−500 Winner winner, +100 legacy), which can cost them milestones and bonus songs until they win a fight.
- Badges are otherwise kept once earned; this is the only migration that removes one. It cannot be undone from the data afterwards, so it needs the legacy badge as the record of who held it.
- The legacy badge never counts towards Winner winner and is not a regular badge, so new users can never earn it.
